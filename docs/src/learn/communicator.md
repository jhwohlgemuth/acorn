# Communicator trail

Goal: turn a project story into clear, researcher-reviewed material for different audiences.

<div class="acorn-growth emoji-growth four-stages" aria-label="Communicator trail from seed to tree"><span>Discover</span><span>Draft</span><span>Review</span><span>Share</span></div>

1. Read [why ACORN describes projects as research objects](../motivation.md) to see how one record connects a project's purpose, people, and outputs.
2. Explore the [research activity data schema (RADS)](../concepts/acorn.md) to find where the title, narrative, contact, media, and related work belong. Draft the story with the researcher who can verify its claims.
3. Use the [`check` guide](../cli-reference/commands/check.md) to understand feedback on prose, links, and missing information. Review the findings with the researcher and revise the source record.
4. Choose a shareable format in the [`export` guide](../cli-reference/commands/export.md), such as a PDF fact sheet or PowerPoint presentation. Review the generated artifact for audience, accuracy, and presentation.

Your finish line is one reviewed project record and a shareable artifact made from it. A teammate can run the commands while you shape the content and review the result.
