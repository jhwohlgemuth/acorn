## Acceptance conclusion

PA-36A is accepted for TEST ONLY materialization. Empty, six-row, correction, 100-row, and 5,000-row batches reached Ready only after bounded source and item rereads reconciled. Invalid payload and missing-predecessor fixtures failed before Ready. Exact-period creation, partial-materialization retry, Ready retry after an uncertain response, overlap, future interval, and malformed timestamp behavior are verified. Both materializers and the seed flow are Off.

The materializer captures a high-water ID and intentionally excludes later IDs from its immutable batch. Detecting a source mutation that occurs between the two secured reads would require timing a controlled write inside an active run; detecting a late in-period row after Ready belongs to PA-37B's full source recheck before finalization. Those injections were not performed. Authorization fails closed before source enumeration by construction, but an end-to-end forbidden run was not available because group 42 contains only the owner account. These limits do not change the accepted PA-36A outcome; PA-37B retains the mutation, late-arrival, and finalizer-denial gates before any Approved state can become visible.
