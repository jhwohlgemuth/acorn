[Verified audience](_assets/sharepoint/participant-access/pilot-two-user-access.txt) records the roles. Group rollout is deferred until the audience expands. For that larger rollout, contributors should be managed through one Microsoft Entra security group, assigned User access to the app and run-only access to its catalog/intake flows where required. They do not need SharePoint list/library membership. Group membership is the routine onboarding/offboarding control; do not add contributors as app or flow co-owners.

The SharePoint actions use a designated owner connection. Teams notifications use the designated notification connection. The Power Apps caller remains the submitter: the existing intake extracts identity from platform trigger headers and rejects a payload whose submitter email differs. Exact replay requires byte-identical original payload, including the submitter. Site/list/library destinations remain fixed in the flows; no user-supplied storage path is admitted. Connection identity and authenticated submitter identity are separate concepts.

The form already shows receipt UUID and confirmed/pending export status without a Launch/open-file action in the captured controls. No contributor download button needs removal. Earlier conversational references to an Open receipt link described a possible direct-file interaction; they were not an inventory of the actual current controls. Operators download through the documented SharePoint library path.

# Apply and verify

The owner-saved settings have been verified. Refresh/rebind app flow metadata only if the published app reports stale connection mappings; preserve its recovery key and pending receipts. The owner-context smoke run is supplemented by accepted participant-context submission and collected-file evidence. For future audience changes, repeat catalog loading, submission, exact receipt/export inclusion and notification checks with an intended participant. The current acceptance is specific to this two-person pilot; it is not a tenant-wide entitlement audit.

When a suitable group and permitted environment are available, bind the same group once to the app's User role and any required flow Run-only permissions. Obtain action-time approval for the concrete audience grants. Verify membership addition and removal using approved test accounts and platform propagation, including the flow permission boundary. Do not remove the working individual share until group access is proven. Contributors receive no flow edit, connection management or direct list/library privileges. Changing group membership should then be the only routine contributor-management action.

Keep operators/reviewers separate: approve named people for catalog maintenance, receipt review and/or JSONL download as their role requires. Do not assume all reviewers need edit rights. Future shared deployment should use an approved operational account rather than a personal connection, with a named connection owner and renewal process. No premium connector is introduced by this connection-mode change; the current participant acceptance does not establish wider environment sharing capacity or every future user’s entitlement.

# Rollback

If the connection change fails acceptance, record the failure and restore **Provided by run-only user** for the catalog SharePoint and intake SharePoint/Teams settings. Reopen both dialogs to verify. This returns the previous behavior, including the requirement for caller storage permissions; it is not a fix for the participant's existing 403. Do not delete receipts, clear browser recovery or resubmit with a new UUID to address an access error.
