# Industry and research drone miniatures

Thirty-four original illustrative GLBs (29 aircraft, three ground stations, one delivery droid and a generic landing pad) cover surveying, infrastructure inspection, autonomy development and small-robot research. This is a representative selection, not a statistically established worldwide top-12 ranking. Public sources do not establish a full ORNL aircraft inventory or relative usage counts. Only Strelka has an ORNL-specific association in the sources below; inclusion of other platforms does not imply ORNL ownership or endorsement.

Each file has an adjacent editable `.model.json` recipe. Geometry is authored from scratch as a simplified silhouette, with ORNL colors, no vendor logos and no imported manufacturer meshes. These are visualization assets, not engineering CAD, aerodynamic geometry, sensor calibration models or manufacturer-accurate dimensions. Rotor layouts, landing gear, camera positions, protective cages and major wing/body forms distinguish the platforms. Propellers are static. The model and pedestal are separate nodes. Aircraft and supporting components are independent picker entries, each fitted to its own display pedestal, except the standalone ground-level landing pad; they are not a shared-scale assembly. Dock lids, exposed reel and cable are illustrative display poses, not mechanical schematics.

Added aircraft and support assets start at revision 1. Avata 2, BRINC Responder Station, Khronos Ground Station and Zipline Delivery Droid are revision 2 after visual revisions; EVO Max 4T, Mavic 3 Enterprise and ANAFI Ai are revision 6; seven other original aircraft are revision 5; eBee X and WingtraOne GEN II retain revision 4. Their shapes are illustrative interpretations of the linked references.

Sources were consulted November 26, 2026. ORNL's [environmental and security drone research article](https://www.ornl.gov/news/drones-give-birds-eye-view-collecting-environmental-and-security-data) supplies use-case context, rather than a model-by-model fleet list. The manufacturer pages support platform identity and broad form/use, not comparative popularity. No external files or network requests are needed to display these GLBs.

Re-author with `python3 crates/acorn-web/scripts/model-collection.py --category drone`, then run the builder and validator described in the [asset guide](../README.md).

The [quality review](../quality-review.json) records each model’s disposition. Avata’s three-blade rotors follow the [official manual](https://dl.djicdn.com/downloads/DJI_Avata_2/UM/20240424/DJI_Avata_2_User_Manual_v1.0_en.pdf); Crazyflie’s brushed-motor construction follows [Bitcraze’s FAQ](https://www.bitcraze.io/support/f-a-q/).
