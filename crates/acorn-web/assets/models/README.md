# Geospatial model assets

This managed library contains **77 GLBs**: 31 research objects (including markers for all 17 DOE laboratories), 6 plain markers, 6 U.S. landmarks and 34 drone-related assets (29 aircraft, three ground stations, a delivery droid and a landing pad). The separate **24-piece game collection** is delivered under `/Users/o9w/dev/risk/assets/models/`, with twelve Risk pieces in `risk/` and twelve generic board-game pieces in `other/`. Models follow the crate-owned asset pattern used by migrations and prompts: versioned files with editable sources, stable IDs, provenance and deterministic output.

Models retain ACORN's shared ORNL greens, blues and neutrals. Laboratory markers use each laboratory’s brand color for the pin body with white emblem geometry; original artwork and color sources are retained with their provenance. At the user's request, orange roles now use natural earth finishes: burnt umber (`#9A563A`), oxide red (`#A5543B`), warm ochre (`#9F794B`), canyon clay (`#95674C`), weathered sandstone (`#A9755B`), limestone (`#C2AF90`) and antique brass (`#9B804A`). The supercomputer uses bright cyan (`#00E5FF`) illuminated accent strips in place of yellow/ochre. These are illustrative material colors, not measured samples. [palette.json](palette.json) distinguishes official brand values from natural finishes; material factors are converted from sRGB to glTF linear RGB and retain color names/hex values in `extras`.

The detail pass adds mechanical vents, fans, fasteners, cables and lenses; architectural columns, stone courses, stairs and trim; terrain strata; and clothing and game-piece details. Laboratory is a compact futuristic pavilion with a glass instrument atrium. DOE National Laboratory is a larger campus with a five-story central tower, two curved research wings, glass skybridges and an illuminated scientific atrium. User Facility is a futuristic research campus: three-level curved laboratory wings, a transparent domed atrium, sweeping white roof ribbons, ORNL Mist illuminated edges, a visible central scientific instrument, a landscaped entrance bridge and an illuminated storage-ring halo on steel supports above the roof. Collaboration is three connected spheres. Research Team uses three minimal people with spherical heads and smooth rounded shoulders in ORNL Green, Infinity blue and Graphite around a shared faceted dodecahedron and circular foundation. Research Project is a computer workstation: a monitor with line, bar, donut and network visualizations, stand, rear vents/ports, keyboard, mouse, tower, open book, stacked papers, pencil and mug. The chemistry setup and task board have been removed. Embedded roughness grain and material-specific metal/paint/stone finishes provide surface variation. These remain illustrative miniatures rather than photogrammetric replicas. The separate [ORNL East Quad campus](research/ornl-campus.md) is a detailed, reference-based architectural scene: six recognizable buildings, modeled glazing and facade trim, rooftop equipment, roads, the pond, trees and parked cars. Its approximate campus scale is checked against public footprints; it is not a surveyed or photogrammetric model. The compact [ORNL Marker](research/ornl-marker.glb) is a beveled ORNL Green location pin with a raised white ORNL oak leaf emblem on both faces, with a Graphite metallic rim. It has no pedestal: the point sits at the origin for globe placement. Its 3.37-meter authoring height is illustrative and can be scaled by the caller. The leaf outline is reproduced from the bundled [ORNL white wordmark](../../../acorn-cli/assets/images/logo_ornl_white.svg) and extruded as geometry. Marker revision 3 removes the acronym and enlarges the oak leaf; it contains no text or plaque. The [ACORN Logo](research/acorn-logo.glb) extrudes the canonical [ACORN artwork](../../../../docs/src/_assets/acorn.svg): the cap and curved stem, four open cap slots, pointed nut and curved body notch. Its brown `#80350E` is recorded as the ACORN Brown custom accent. Separate `cap-and-stem` and `nut-body` nodes retain the logo’s gap, with beveled edges and no background tile or pedestal. The authoring height is 3.4 meters and can be scaled when placed.

Cargo crate packages exclude this entire directory and all model generation, validation, preview and viewer tools through workspace exclusions. `acorn-web` inherits them and remains `publish = false`. These authoring assets are also not automatically staged into browser releases.

## File and coordinate contract

Keep each `.model.json` beside its `.glb`, and commit the regenerated catalog. Increment revision when appearance, geometry, materials or coordinates change. The detail/earth-finish collection is revision 4; the supercomputer, redesigned Research Project, Laboratory and DOE National Laboratory are revision 5; User Facility is revision 6; the simplified Research Team is revision 7. The additional drone-related assets start at revision 1; Avata 2 and Khronos Ground Station are now revision 2. The quality pass advances ten original drones, Dataset, Software, Field Site and five landmarks to revision 5. The November 27 follow-up advances EVO Max 4T, Mavic 3 Enterprise, ANAFI Ai, Gateway Arch and Hoover Dam to revision 6, U.S. Capitol to revision 5, User Facility to revision 7 and Zipline Delivery Droid to revision 2. The catalog records each current revision. Stable IDs do not change with revision.

Files are self-contained glTF 2.0 GLB with embedded, indexed geometry buffers (bit-identical corners are shared; 16-bit indices unless a primitive exceeds 65,535 vertices) and a deterministic 64×64 roughness texture, with no external textures, URIs or decoder dependencies. Laboratory markers use shallow white emblem extrusions; names and abbreviations remain in viewer labels only. ORNL also embeds five 1024×1024 color textures for brick, roofing, concrete, asphalt and lawn. Units are meters, +Y is up, +Z front, and the origin is at the bottom center. Most assets use fitted miniature dimensions, not surveyed geographic footprints or actual aircraft sizes. ORNL retains approximate meter dimensions with its horizontal axes aligned to the public campus map; it has no automatic georeferenced placement. Display pedestals, when present, use a separate `pedestal` node from the `body`; supercomputer racks retain their separate nodes. The separate game collection uses standalone forms without display pedestals. Risk base symbols are separately addressable. Hide the display pedestal when using a standalone object if appropriate.

The geometry and authoring code are original MIT-licensed work under the repository license, except the ORNL marker’s leaf contour and the other laboratories’ official logo artwork, which retain their owners’ rights and are not relicensed under MIT. The [logo source manifest](designs/laboratory-logos/catalog.json) preserves attribution, hashes and source links. Drone references guide simplified silhouettes; no manufacturer mesh was imported. Generic laboratory symbols do not identify real facilities. [Original design sheets and prompts](designs/) preserve the concept references; their earlier color choices and concept-only brief are superseded by the generated models and current palette. None of these assets implement game rules or bind an object to a real-world location.

## Browse the collection

From the ACORN repository root:

```sh
npm --prefix crates/acorn-web run models
```

Open [localhost:8085](http://127.0.0.1:8085/) and refresh the picker after generation. It starts with the supercomputer. Previous/Next buttons on the preview follow the Model dropdown’s filtered order across folder sections, stopping at either end. With the current model outside the filtered list, Next opens the first result and Previous opens the last. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, or use Front, Back, Top and Reset view. Focus the preview for arrow-key orbit, `+`/`−` zoom and `R` reset. The single-page viewer uses ACORN’s shared application stylesheet and theme control, without a left navbar or page title. The Model picker groups assets alphabetically: Drones, Landmarks, Markers and Research for ACORN; Other and Risk when viewing the separate game collection. Markers combines all 17 laboratory pins with the six plain variants, including laboratory files retained in `research/`. The Markers collection filter uses the same grouping; asset IDs and download paths are unchanged. Collection/search filters, the picker, preview and asset inspector remain available; preview controls use the shared compact choice-control styles. Dark/light theme choices persist locally. Filters narrow the picker while leaving the current preview visible until another model is selected. Failed selections retain the last successful model.

The server only exposes the viewer, shared ACORN stylesheet/icons, catalog, GLBs and installed Cesium files over loopback; rendering uses no ion token or network service. Install the existing Cesium dependency with `npm ci --prefix crates/acorn-web/geospatial` if missing. Ctrl+C stops the server. To browse Risk separately:

```sh
node crates/acorn-web/scripts/model-viewer.mjs 8086 /Users/o9w/dev/risk/assets/models
```

Open [localhost:8086](http://127.0.0.1:8086/). Any model root with the same catalog contract can be supplied as the second server argument.

## Author, rebuild and validate

Recipes are the canonical editable source. The collection generator can re-author a whole category from procedural designs; this overwrites that category's recipes, so preserve manual recipe edits before using it. The supercomputer remains its own hand-authored recipe. The ACORN logo, ORNL campus, ORNL marker and additional laboratory/plain markers have separate authoring scripts so category regeneration preserves their dedicated recipes:

```sh
python3 crates/acorn-web/scripts/model-acorn-logo.py
python3 crates/acorn-web/scripts/model-ornl.py
python3 crates/acorn-web/scripts/model-ornl-marker.py
python3 crates/acorn-web/scripts/model-logo-emblems.py
python3 crates/acorn-web/scripts/model-lab-markers.py
python3 crates/acorn-web/scripts/build-models.py
```

```sh
python3 crates/acorn-web/scripts/model-collection.py --category drone
python3 crates/acorn-web/scripts/build-models.py
python3 crates/acorn-web/scripts/build-models.py --check
npm install --prefix /tmp/acorn-model-validation --ignore-scripts --no-audit --no-fund gltf-validator@2.0.0-dev.3.10
node crates/acorn-web/scripts/validate-models.mjs /tmp/acorn-model-validation/node_modules/gltf-validator
node crates/acorn-web/scripts/model-collection-smoke.mjs crates/acorn-web/assets/models drone
npm --prefix crates/acorn-web run test:models-viewer
```

Drone files live in `drones/`; stable IDs and the generator category remain `drone` to preserve existing references. Use ACORN's generator for the active `drone`, `landmarks` and `research` categories. The current simplified `other` and `risk` designs are owned by the independent scripts in `/Users/o9w/dev/risk/scripts`; run those generators and the builder from the Risk directory, as documented in its `assets/models/README.md`. Older shared helper implementations retained in ACORN are not the source for the revised Risk pieces. The main generators and GLB builder use the Python standard library. The optional logo-mask tracing step uses Pillow. Saved recipes do not require this authoring dependency. The ACORN logo and laboratory marker authoring scripts additionally invoke Node (`NODE_BINARY`, or `node` on PATH) with Earcut from the installed Cesium dependencies to triangulate logo contours and holes; it fetches nothing at runtime. GLB rebuilds from the saved recipe do not need this extra authoring step. Mesh helpers support extruded concave profiles, lathed bodies, beams, terrain, spheres, tori and beveled boxes; `model-detail.py` adds category-specific construction details; the builder batches triangles by node and material with deterministic normals, projected UVs and an embedded roughness texture.

The builder's `--check` compares regenerated bytes/catalog metadata without writing. Khronos validation also verifies catalog counts/hashes, embedded resources and every material against the recorded ORNL palette, laboratory brand colors, natural finishes and custom accents. The validator accepts an optional model root and optional report destination after the validator-module path. The collection smoke harness loads every selected GLB in Cesium, rejects external requests/page errors and saves front, rear, top and 220-pixel views. It accepts an optional category after the model root. `--contacts-only` refreshes contact sheets from existing per-model renders. Playwright and its browser binary must already be installed through the existing web tooling. Inspect the saved renders; successful loading alone is not visual acceptance.

## Acceptance and runtime integration

The November 26, 2026 quality review inspected all **63 GLBs** (51 ACORN and 12 Risk) in front, rear, top and small views, improving **22 models**. The historical [per-model review](quality-review.json) records findings, changes, reference sources, hashes at that review and limitations. Changes cover drone blade counts, coreless-motor construction, shaped shells and attached camera mounts; darker motor finishes; a coiled tether; modern Dataset, Software and Field Site models; corrected Gateway Arch sections, bridge trusses, eroded canyon terrain, Hoover Dam and SLS-inspired launch geometry; and improved Risk cavalry and artillery. The reviewed artifacts passed deterministic regeneration, Khronos validation with zero errors/warnings, finite-position/unit-normal checks and local Cesium rendering at the time of that review. These are original stylized miniatures, not dimensionally accurate manufacturer or survey models.

The November 27, 2026 follow-up reviewed the 53 ACORN GLBs then present, including the landing pad and ORNL campus that the earlier review omitted, and improved **8 models**: Hoover Dam's arch now bulges toward the reservoir instead of downstream; the Zipline droid's rear fan is strut-mounted and its side thrusters are symmetric; EVO Max 4T, Mavic 3 Enterprise and ANAFI Ai use short motor feet in place of tall posts, and ANAFI Ai's stereo cameras are compact; Gateway Arch drops its loose plaza tiles; the U.S. Capitol has a taller colonnaded drum and dome; and User Facility gains a storage-ring halo that separates it from Laboratory at globe size. The builder now writes indexed geometry, cutting the library by 47% without changing bounds or triangle counts. ACORN Logo and ORNL Marker were added separately and are outside that review.

The November 27 laboratory-marker revision removes all plaques and lettering from the 17 named pins. The additional markers are revision 3 with laboratory-specific body colors; ORNL is revision 3 with an enlarged oak leaf and its existing primary green. All 17 pass byte-for-byte recipe regeneration, closed-emblem mesh checks, Khronos validation and front/rear/top/small Cesium rendering. All 77 catalogued GLBs pass Khronos validation with zero errors or warnings. The color revision preserves all 17 marker geometries; the other 61 GLBs, including ORNL and the six plain variants, remain byte-for-byte unchanged. The whole-library `--check` still encounters a pre-existing recipe/export mismatch at `drones/autel-evo-max-4t.glb`; this marker revision does not resolve it. [Marker verification](previews/laboratory-markers-acceptance.json) records these boundaries.

Collection verification uses deterministic regeneration, Khronos glTF Validator 2.0.0-dev.3.10, and metadata and palette checks. Installed Cesium 1.147.0/Chromium loaded all models without external requests or page errors. Front, rear, top and small-size renders were inspected; [validation.json](validation.json) and category acceptance records under [previews](previews/) retain artifact hashes and results. The standalone viewer's picker, failure recovery, orbit/zoom, keyboard controls, downloads, search/category filters, persistent dark/light themes and mobile layout passed its focused checks. Cargo package listings exclude GLBs and associated tooling.

The current ACORN collection contains 77 models; the separate Risk collection contains 24, for 101 total across both libraries. The twelve generic game pieces and their previews moved from ACORN to Risk without changing their stable IDs. Subsequent game-piece revisions are authored and validated in Risk. Yellowstone, Statue of Liberty and all Wonder models have been removed from the active inventory. Historical concept sheets remain as design references.

Live Geospatial-page integration, terrain placement, repeated-instance performance, memory budgets, distance switching and accessible production selection remain with ABW-37 after ABW-18 measurements. Asset-level rendering does not accept those production gates. Future browser packaging should copy only catalog-listed runtime files using base-path-safe URLs; recipes, concepts and previews remain authoring material.
