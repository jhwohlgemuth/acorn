Oakley

Oakley lives in a corner of the ACORN browser. Click it to open a chat with the ACORN agent, and watch it for news about your research activity data. It's a single-color acorn with ridge waves on its cap, two pill eyes, and an antenna light. It has no mouth; the eyes and the antenna do all the talking.

Anatomy

Six parts, one color

Everything is drawn in one ink. The gap under the cap, the waves, and the eyes are cut out of the shape, so the page background shows through.

States

What Oakley is doing

A state lasts until the agent's status changes. Only idle, thinking, and speaking loop.

Actions

One-shot reactions

An action plays once on top of the current state, then Oakley returns to that state. Click any avatar to replay it.

Sizes

From hero to favicon

Dark, Light, neutral ink, and USWDS. The waves read down to 32px and fade into the cap below that. The favicon drops them. The Retro band shows the Pixel style at whole-pixel multiples of its 26-unit frame.

Rules

Keeping Oakley Oakley

No mouth

Expression comes from the eyes and the antenna light only.

Nothing nags

Has news plays twice, about six seconds, then settles with the antenna lit. Actions play once.

Respect reduced motion

With reduced motion on, every animation stops and Oakley holds each pose.

A nod, not a logo

The ridge waves hint at Oak Ridge. Oakley never copies ORNL's logo, wordmark, or official marks.

Ink and light

Ink · accent Ink · neutral Thinking News

Simple shapes

Chubby nut, wide cap, curled stem. No leaf, since it made the shape read as an apple. The Pixel style redraws the same parts on a 20×20 grid and steps every animation by a whole pixel or frame.
